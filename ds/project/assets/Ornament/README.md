The Kyrgyz ornament art from the app's `public/` folder. It is decorative only and always sits behind content.

- `ornament-tile.webp`: the page background layer, stretched over the 430px column at 34% opacity on a `#fdf9f6` ground.
- `ornament-bottom.webp`: a bottom border for hero and empty screens.
- `ornament-side-left.webp` and `ornament-side-right.webp`: mirrored side borders, always used as a pair.

Never recolour, crop into the motif or place text over the dense part of the art.
